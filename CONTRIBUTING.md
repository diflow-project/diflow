# Contributing to DiFlow

Thank you for your interest in contributing to DiFlow. Our community is open to everyone, and we welcome contributions of all sizes.

Ways to contribute include:

- Identifying and reporting issues or bugs.
- Requesting or adding support for a new model or diffusion workflow.
- Suggesting or implementing new features.
- Improving documentation or contributing a how-to guide.

We also believe in the power of community support. Answering questions, reviewing pull requests, and helping other users are highly regarded and beneficial contributions.

More details are coming soon.
